How the bot trades

Buy dips in fixed lots, sell each lot above its own cost -- never on an average, never at a loss. Every parameter below adjusts one piece of that loop.

Lot

One buy of LOT_USD. The book is a list of lots, each tracking its own cost. All sell rules apply per lot, so a cheap lot never subsidizes an expensive one.

Never sell at a loss

The hard rule: a lot only sells when effective proceeds (after fee and slippage) exceed what it cost. A price drop turns lots into inventory waiting for recovery, never into a realized loss.

Grid

Buy a lot every −BUY_STEP_PCT% drop; sell each lot at +SELL_STEP_PCT% above its cost. Asymmetric steps (e.g. 5% buy / 12% sell) buy dips often while extracting a bigger margin on the way out.

Trailing sell

Instead of selling the instant a lot hits its target, track the price peak and sell only when it retraces SELL_TRAIL_PCT% from it — never below the target. Lets winners run further on strong pumps.

Relative ceiling

No buys above the Nth percentile of the coin's own 14-day price history. Prevents the grid from accumulating near local tops; adapts every tick as the market moves.

Price gate

If a price tick jumps more than PRICE_GATE_PCT% from the previous reading, treat it as bad data and skip the tick entirely. Guards against feed glitches and flash-spike decisions.
